## Day-by-Day

---

### Day 1 — Thursday, July 31
**Arrive Tokyo**

- Arrive Haneda or Narita → check in to hotel (Shinjuku or Shibuya recommended as base)
- **Welcome lunch: Udatsu Sushi** — Chef Toru Udatsu, world-class sushiya
  - ⚠️ *Counter-style omakase — confirm max capacity for 8–12 people. May require two consecutive sittings or a group reservation call. Book 2–3 months in advance minimum.*
- Easy evening: ramen, walk Kabukicho or Shibuya Crossing, get on Japan time
- **Neighborhood:** Shinjuku or Shibuya

---

### Day 2 — Friday, August 1
**Tokyo: Tokyo BunBun (Partner)**

- Full day with **Tokyo BunBun** — confirmed partner. Allow the full day; do not schedule travel around this.
- Evening: dinner in Tokyo, wherever the experience lands you
- **Stay:** Tokyo

---

### Day 3 — Saturday, August 2
**Tokyo: Bonsai Racing (Partner)**

- Full day with Bonsai Racing partner
- Evening: late dinner, Shinjuku or wherever the day ends

---

### Day 4 — Sunday, August 3
**Tokyo → Morioka | Sansa Festival**

- **Morning:** Tohoku Shinkansen Tokyo Station → **Morioka** (~2h20m, JR Pass) — reserve as group block
- **Afternoon in Morioka** — NYT #2 of 52 Places 2023; don't brush this off:
  - Walk the east bank of the **Kitakami River** toward Seinan Ohashi bridge — Mt. Iwate rising over the city
  - **Morioka Castle Ruins** (Iwate Park) — 5-min walk from station; park grounds, elevated views
  - **Azumaya** — all-you-can-eat wanko soba (you slam small bowls until you tap out; competitive, genuinely fun for a group)
  - **Nagasawa Coffee** or **Clammbon** — two of the most serious coffee shops in Tohoku
  - **Johnny's jazz bar** — 40+ years open, owner runs a record label from the back; find it
- **Evening:** 🥁 **Morioka Sansa Festival** — world's largest taiko drum festival, runs every August
  - Streets fill with taiko drummers, fue flute players, and chanters; mass procession through downtown
  - At the close of the night: join the dancers. Not metaphorically — literally join the procession
  - ⚠️ *Confirm 2026 exact dates; typically August 1–4 — August 3 should be Day 3 of the festival*
- **Stay:** Morioka — Daiwa Roynet Morioka Ekimae / Richmond Hotel / Dormy Inn (all station-adjacent; book 4–6 rooms)

---

### Day 5 — Monday, August 4
**Morioka → Aomori | Nebuta Night 3 (First Night)**

- **Morning:** Tohoku Shinkansen Morioka → **Aomori** (~1 hr, JR Pass)
- **Afternoon:** Check in, decompress — this is the first Nebuta night so arrive with energy to spare
  - **A-FACTORY** (waterfront, apple cider tasting) if time allows
  - **Rassera Land** (Aoi-umi Park) — see floats up close at the builder huts before the parade; open through August 7
- **6:45 pm:** 🎏 **Nebuta Night 3** — 20 large nebutas; first parade night for the group
  - Haneto costumes available for rental (~¥4,000 for basic set) — anyone can join the dancers
- **Stay:** Aomori City ⚠️ *8–12 people together: private guesthouse or hotel block booked months in advance*

---

### Day 6 — Tuesday, August 5
**Aomori: Cultural Day + Nokke-don + Nebuta Night 4**

- **Early morning (7am):** 🍣 **Nokke-don at Aomori Gyosai Center (Furukawa Market)** — buy a bowl of rice, choose toppings from stalls (scallops, tuna, salmon roe, squid, crab)
- **Mid-morning:** **Nebuta no Ie Wa Rasse** — full-sized float museum outside Aomori Station; see how floats are wired and papered; try on Haneto costumes
- Optional: **ASPAM** observation deck (5 min from Wa Rasse) — bay and mountain panorama
- **Afternoon:** **Aomori Museum of Art** — Yoshitomo Nara's "Aomori Dog" (*No Time to Die* connection); **Sannai-Maruyama** (Jomon-period UNESCO site, bus ~20 min or taxi ~10 min)
- **6:45 pm:** 🎏 **Nebuta Night 4** — 20 large nebutas

---

### Day 7 — Wednesday, August 6
**Hirosaki Morning → Goshogawara Overnight | Tachineputa Festival**

- **Morning:** Train to **Hirosaki** (~1 hr, Ou Line, JR Pass)
  - **Hirosaki Castle** — one of Japan's 12 remaining original Edo-period fortresses; 2,600 trees on the grounds
  - **Hirosaki MOCA** — opened 2020 in a converted 19th-century brick brewery; Café & Restaurant Brick for cider
  - *The Hirosaki Neputa Festival also runs Aug 1–7 — fan-shaped floats, more somber tone than Aomori's. May catch a daytime procession.*
- **Early afternoon:** Train Hirosaki → **Goshogawara** (~40 min on Gonō Line via Kawabe; JR Pass)
  - **Tachineputa no Yakata (Museum)** — the three permanent 23m giants live here year-round; 5-min walk from station
  - Explore the town, eat dinner before the parade
- **19:00–21:00:** 🎏 **Goshogawara Tachineputa Festival**
  - **Floats: 23 meters tall, 19 tons** — you'll see the tops above low buildings before they round the corner
  - **15 floats total**, all glowing from within; themed around deities, demons, Japanese folklore
  - **The chant: "Yattemare! Yattemare!"**
  - **Look DOWN, not up** — they're on wheels, pulled by rope teams at front, pushed/steered from behind through narrow streets lined with power lines
  - ⭐ **Best position: a corner on the parade route** — watch the full push/pull/pivot turn of a 19-ton float down a narrow side street
  - **Seating:** Free standing; reserved seats ¥3,000–3,500/person — book from ~April 20 (Goshogawara Chamber of Commerce: 0173-35-2121) ⚠️ *Book for all 8–12 people in one block*
- **Stay overnight in Goshogawara** ⚠️ **Do not plan to return to Aomori tonight**
  - The last confirmed train connecting to Aomori departs Goshogawara at **19:33** — before the parade starts. There is a 21:21 train toward Hirosaki but no confirmed onward connection to Aomori that night. For 8–12 people, this is not a risk worth taking.
  - Book guesthouse or business hotel in Goshogawara for this night

---

### Day 8 — Thursday, August 7
**Goshogawara → Hachinohe → Aomori | Nebuta Award Night**

- **Morning:** Train from Goshogawara → **Hachinohe** (change at Aomori or via Hirosaki; ~2 hrs, JR Pass — confirm routing on Jorudan)
  - **Kabushima Shrine** — rebuilt 2020; 30,000+ black-tailed gulls (*umineko*) still present in early August, nearly tame. 92 stairs, ocean views. Umbrellas at the bottom for bird coverage. ⭐ Strange and wonderful.
  - **Tanesashi Coast** — rugged Pacific coastline, rock formations, coastal trail
  - **Miroku Yokocho** — old-school izakaya alley, fresh local seafood (good lunch option)
  - 🚌 *100-yen Umineko Bus* runs the coast — flat fare regardless of distance
- **Afternoon:** Train back to **Aomori** (~40 min from Hachinohe, JR Pass)
- **6:45 pm:** 🎏 **Nebuta Night 5 — Award-Winning Float Parade** — competition winners' final procession; widely considered the most spectacular evening of the festival

---

### Day 9 — Friday, August 8
**Nebuta Finale — Marine Procession + Fireworks**

- Morning: free in Aomori — rest, final market run, pack
- **1:00 pm:** 🎏 Final daytime parade (~15 nebutas)
- Afternoon: store luggage at station
- **7:15 pm:** 🎆 **Nebuta Marine Procession** — floats placed on boats in Aomori Bay; floating nebuta lanterns released on the water; **Aomori Fireworks Festival**
- Late night: Pre-pack for Shinkansen and limited express travel tomorrow

---

### Day 10 — Saturday, August 9
**Aomori → Sapporo (via Hakodate)**

> ⚠️ **Weather note:** Hokkaido August is not a guaranteed paradise — multi-day rain stretches happen even mid-summer. Build some indoor fallbacks (Beer Museum, Shiroi Koibito Park, Nijo Market) for gray days.

- **Morning:** Hokkaido Shinkansen from Shin-Aomori to Shin-Hakodate-Hokuto (~1 hr), passing through the **Seikan Tunnel** (54km undersea).
- **Hakodate Stopover:** 
  - **Morning market** (famous for fresh crab — try it half grilled, half sashimi)
  - **Goryokaku star fort (1864)** — Western-style bastion
- **Afternoon:** Limited express train to **Sapporo** (~3.5 hrs)
- **Late:** Check in to Sapporo hotel. Evening dinner in Susukino.
- **Stay:** Sapporo (Susukino or Odori area)

---

### Day 11 — Sunday, August 10
**Sapporo City**

- **Nijo Market** (opens 7 am) — earliest possible: fresh *uni ikura donburi* (sea urchin + salmon roe on rice), haggling OK (rare in Japan)
- **Sapporo Beer Museum** — free entry, historic Akarenga brick building; tastings include Hokkaido-only seasonal releases; dinner reservation available in the beer garden (all-you-can-eat Genghis Khan lamb BBQ)
- **Odori Park** — check if **Hokkai Bon Odori** has started (traditional Obon circle dance, free, 18:00–21:00) ⚠️ *Typically runs mid-August — might be just after our dates; confirm 2026 schedule*
- Late: **Susukino** — miso ramen at Ramen Alley, nightlife, soup curry
- **Stay:** Sapporo

---

### Day 12 — Monday, August 11
**Day Trip from Sapporo: Otaru (recommended) or Lake Toya**

- **Otaru** (~35 min by train — or drive for more flexibility):
  - **Otaru Canal** — historic warehouses, walk the banks
  - **Blue Cave cruise** — boat tour to brilliant sea caves near the Otaru coast; one of the most photogenic spots in Hokkaido
  - **Sushiya-dori** (Sushi Street) — omakase lunch; Otaru is legitimately one of Japan's top sushi destinations
  - **Yoichi Whisky Distillery** (~25 min further by train) — Nikka's original distillery; tours + tasting
- **OR Lake Toya** (~1.5 hrs by express or ~1 hr by car): caldera lake, Mount Usu ropeway, **nightly fireworks from the lakeshore** (summer tradition); onsen hotels with lake views
- Back to Sapporo for the night

---

### Day 13 — Tuesday, August 12
**Sapporo → Tokyo (Fly)**

- Morning: last Sapporo activity (Maruyama Park, Moerenuma Park if weather good)
- Afternoon: fly **New Chitose Airport (CTS) → Haneda (HND)** — approx. 1.5 hrs
  - ⚠️ *Book early — August flights fill fast. Budget ~¥10,000–20,000 one way.*
- Arrive Tokyo mid-afternoon, check in
- Evening: ease back in — **Shimokitazawa** (vintage shops, small live venues) or **Koenji** for a low-key start
- **Stay:** Tokyo (Shinjuku or Shibuya)

---

### Day 14 — Wednesday, August 13
**Tokyo: Final Day — Shopping & Send-Off**

- Morning: **Koenji** (underground thrifting — most concentrated vintage stores in Tokyo, alternative/punk vibe) or **Akihabara** (electronics, merch)
- Lunch: ⚠️ *One final bucket-list meal — book in advance*
- Afternoon: final shopping, pack, luggage to hotel storage
- Evening: **Shibuya Crossing** at dusk → farewell dinner → late flight or next-day departure
- ⚠️ *Departure date — August 13 evening or August 14 morning?*

## Group Logistics

### Group Size: 8–12 People
- All restaurant bookings need advance reservations — especially in Aomori during Nebuta and in Sapporo
- Reserved festival seating (Goshogawara, Nebuta) must be booked as a block — do not assume availability walk-up for 8–12
- **Bonsai Racing and Tokyo BunBun:** Confirm both partners can accommodate the full group size before finalizing dates
- Shinkansen seat reservations: book as a group block at JR ticket office; free with JR Pass but must be done explicitly

### Luggage Forwarding (Takuhaibin)
- **Strategy:** Send all main bags from Tokyo hotel → Sapporo hotel before leaving for Aomori
- **Service:** Yamato (Kuroneko) or Sagawa — ask hotel front desk to handle labels and pickup
- **Timing:** Send at least 2 days before you need them in Sapporo (send morning of Day 3 or night before)
- **Cost:** ~¥2,000–2,500 per large bag; weight limit ~25 kg per bag
- **Requirement:** You need your Sapporo hotel name and address confirmed before sending
- You carry **day packs only** for the entire 6-night Aomori segment
- ⚠️ *Confirm single Tokyo hotel for both opening and closing segments, or send from closing-segment hotel*
